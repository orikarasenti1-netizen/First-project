# MailSeal: live business model

Update this weekly. **Cash received** is tracked separately from everything else.

## Unit economics

| Item | Fix (founding) | Fix (regular) | Watch | Partner domain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price | ₪350 / $99 | ₪490 / $149 | ₪69 / $19 per month | ₪290 / $79 |
| Payment fees (bank transfer 0%; PayPal ≈ 4–5% **[assumption, check your PayPal fee page]**; Fiverr 20%) | ₪0–18 | ₪0–25 | ₪0–4 | ₪0–15 |
| Other variable cost | ₪0 | ₪0 | ₪0 (GitHub Actions free tier) | ₪0 |
| **Gross profit** | ₪332–350 | ₪465–490 | ₪65–69 | ₪275–290 |
| Your time | ~1.5 h (30-min session, 2 × 10-min policy steps, ~30 min admin) | ~1.5 h | ~10 min/mo (alerts only) | ~1 h |
| **Effective hourly** | ≈ ₪230/h | ≈ ₪320/h | n/a | ≈ ₪280/h |

- **Fixed monthly costs:** ₪0. Optional own domain ≈ ₪60–100/yr, bought only with your approval.
- **Break-even:** the first sale (no fixed costs).
- **Acquisition cost:** ₪0 cash; ~10 min of your time per personalised send. At an assumed 10% close rate on warm/partner contacts, that's ~100 min per customer.
- **Concentration risk:** partner channel. One agency could become >50% of revenue; don't discount below ₪250/domain.
- **Refund assumption:** 10% of Fix sales (guarantee). **Watch churn assumption:** 5%/month.
